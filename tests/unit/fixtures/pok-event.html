"100 Facts You Probably Didn't Know About London"
With Jonnie Fielding (Bowl of Chalk)

Details:

For the last 15 years, Jonnie Fielding (better known as 'Bowl of Chalk') a dedicated 'Curistorian', has been uncovering all he can about London, its quirks, its secrets and oddities and telling people about them on his regular walking tours. Two and a half years ago, he started to put his knowledge on Instagram and TikTok in the form of bite sized fun London facts, amassing quite a following in the process. Just last week he published a book (Harper Collins) entitled Why is Downing Street Painted Black? and 364 other fun London facts which as the name would suggest is packed full of 365 (and probably actually a few more) fun facts about London, like have you ever tried to find Bond Street? (spoiler alert ...it doesn't exist) or why there will never be 13 diners at The Savoy and other minutiae and details of this great City people often overlook.

James Corden said “Jonnie made me see London through a whole new set of eyes – the beauty in every corner of it. He is full of intrigue, facts and fun and has been a huge part of my love for the place I now call home”, whilst The Times said of Jonnie, “The man is conduit for the city’s soul, distilling its chaotic history into digestible, engaging narratives.”
Come along, grab a drink and hear Jonnie wax lyrical about London and if you're lucky ...he might even tell you why Downing Street is Painted Black.

What is Pints of Knowledge?

Pints of Knowledge is a series of public talks where experts, researchers, and big thinkers share fascinating ideas all in the relaxed setting of a pub. We bring brilliant minds and curious audiences together over a pint, covering topics from science and politics to history, art, and beyond.
